## 👋 Who I Am

I'm a Computer Science Engineer specializing in backend engineering and full-stack development. I build web applications using **Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Next.js, Node.js, Express.js and FastAPI, with PostgreSQL and MongoDB** for data management. My experience also includes **Redis, Docker** and designing **REST APIs**.

Beyond development, I have a foundation in data structures and algorithms, computer science fundamentals and system design. I'm interested in building reliable software, solving engineering problems and understanding what goes into designing and developing applications beyond just their features.
